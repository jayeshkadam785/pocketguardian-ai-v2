Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Last updated: July 2026

1. What We Collect

We collect your email address (for login), emergency contact names and phone numbers you add, and your device's live location only when you trigger an SOS, share your location, or enable live tracking. We never access your location in the background without your action.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to operate safety features: sending your location to your saved contacts during an emergency, displaying your contact list, and enabling live tracking links you choose to share. We do not sell or share your data with third parties or advertisers.

3. Data Security

Your account data is protected using Row-Level Security, meaning only you can access your own contacts and safety logs — not other users, and not us, through the app interface. Passwords are securely hashed and never stored in plain text.

4. Your Rights

You may update or delete your emergency contacts at any time from within the app. To request full account deletion, contact the developer via the details in this app's GitHub repository.

5. Terms of Use

This app is provided as a hackathon project ("Innovate 4 Impact: AI SDG Global Hackathon 2026") on a best-effort basis. While designed to assist in emergencies, it does not replace calling official emergency services (police, ambulance) directly when possible. The developers are not liable for message delivery failures due to network, carrier, or third-party service issues beyond the app's control.

6. Third-Party Services

This app uses Supabase for authentication and data storage, and Vercel for hosting. Location-sharing features rely on your device's native SMS and WhatsApp apps, which are subject to their own respective privacy policies.
