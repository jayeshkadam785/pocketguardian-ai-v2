Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Last updated: August 2026

1. What We Collect

We collect your email (login), emergency contact details, live location during SOS/tracking, and — only if you enable them — voice audio for Voice SOS detection, motion data for Shake/Anomaly detection, and a paired wearable's connection state. Nothing is captured in the background without your action.

2. How We Use Your Data

Data powers safety features only: alerting your contacts, journey check-ins, tactical routing, and your safety score. We do not sell or share data with advertisers.

3. Data Security

Row-Level Security ensures only you can access your own contacts, logs, and journeys. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.

4. Your Rights

You can update or delete contacts, disable sensors, and unpair a wearable at any time. Contact the developer via GitHub for full account deletion.

5. Terms of Use

Built for IEEE WIE "Innovate 4 Impact" Hackathon 2026 on a best-effort basis. Always call official emergency services directly when possible — this app is a supplement, not a replacement.

6. Third-Party Services

Uses Supabase (auth/data) and Vercel (hosting). SMS/WhatsApp features rely on your device's native apps. Voice SOS uses your browser's on-device speech recognition where available.
